Frequently Asked Questions.

Everything participants need to know before registering for XENORAZZ 2K26.

What is the registration fee?

The registration fee is ₹250 per person.

When and where is XENORAZZ 2K26?

The symposium will be held on Wednesday, 07 October 2026 at DMI Engineering College, Kumarapuram Road, Aralvaimozhi, Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu – 629301.

How do I register?

Use the Register Now button on the website. It opens the official Google registration form.

How can I see the rules for an event?

Open the Events section and select the event card. Its dedicated rules panel will appear below the event cards.

What are the Paper Vistaz prizes?

Paper Vistaz prizes are ₹1,500 for first place, ₹1,000 for second place and ₹500 for third place.

Where can I find the college location?

The Find Us section includes an embedded Google Map and a direct Open in Google Maps button.
