EVENT GUIDELINES

Know the rules. Play fair.

Paper Vistaz

  • Team size: 3–4.
  • 5-minute presentation + 3-minute Q&A.
  • PPT must contain title, introduction, problem statement, existing system, proposed system, methodology, results, conclusion and references.

IntelliChatz

  • Team size: 2–3.
  • Creativity, usability and effective interaction are expected.
  • Complete within the allotted time.
  • Presentation must explain the technology and working process.

Brain Bytz

  • Individual presentation.
  • Theme will be given on the spot.
  • 5 minutes preparation.
  • 1 minute presentation.

Kill The Bug

  • Team size: 1–2.
  • Solve logical, syntactical and runtime errors.
  • Internet access is permitted.

Connectionz

  • Team size: 3–4.
  • Connect the picture clues to form the word.
  • Complete within the given time limit.

Vibez

  • 1–5 members.
  • Props and make-up are allowed.
  • Maximum 5 minutes.
  • Song must be independent; keep a copy on your phone.

Reelz

  • Team size: 3–4.
  • Theme will be given on the spot.
  • 30-second video only.
  • Content speaks.

NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers before you register.

Who can participate?

College students can participate in the national-level symposium. Check individual event team-size requirements before registering.

What is the registration fee?

The registration fee is ₹250 per person.

How do I register?

Use the Register Now button to open the official Google Form and complete the registration details.

Can I participate in more than one event?

Participation across events depends on the event schedule. Please ensure that your selected events do not clash.

Where is the symposium conducted?

DMI Engineering College, Kumarapuram Road, Aralvaimozhi, Kanyakumari District, Tamil Nadu – 629301.

What should I bring?

Bring your college ID, required presentation materials and any permitted event-specific items.

Will prizes and certificates be provided?

Prize details are highlighted on this website. Certificate details will be communicated by the symposium team.

Can I contact the organisers?

Yes. Use the contact section below for the symposium team.
