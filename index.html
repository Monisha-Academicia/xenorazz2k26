Rules & Guidelines

Paper Vistaz

  • Team size: 3–4 members.
  • 5-minute presentation followed by a 3-minute Q&A session.
  • PPT must contain: Base Paper, Title, Introduction, Problem Statement, Existing System, Proposed System, Methodology, Results, Conclusion and References.

IntelliChatz

  • Team size: 2–3 members.
  • Creativity, usability and effective interaction are expected.
  • Complete the task within the allotted time.
  • Presentation must include the technology used and the working process.

Brain Bytz

  • Individual participation.
  • Theme will be given on the spot.
  • 5 minutes preparation time.
  • 1 minute presentation.

Kill The Bug

  • Team size: 1–2 members.
  • Solve logical, syntactical and runtime errors.
  • Internet access is limited.

Connectionz

  • Team size: 3–4 members.
  • Connect the pictures to form the word.
  • Complete the challenge within the time limit.

Vibez

  • 1–5 members.
  • Props are allowed.
  • Maximum duration: 5 minutes.
  • Makeup is allowed.
  • Keep a copy of the song on your phone.

Reelz

  • Team size: 3–4 members.
  • Theme will be given on the spot.
  • 30-second videos only.
  • Content speaks for itself.
